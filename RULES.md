# Our algorithm rules

Our feed is a small teaching model made by students. It is **NOT** the real algorithm of any app.

1. **Points.** Every topic has a score. 👀 Keep watching +3. ❤️ Like +2. 🔁 Share +3. ⏭️ Skip −1. 🚫 Not interested −4.
2. **Choosing.** The next post comes from the topic with the most points. A very popular post gets +1. A tiny bit of chance breaks ties.
3. **Variety.** Never two posts by the same creator in a row. Never more than two posts of the same topic in a row.
4. **Explore.** Every 5th post comes from the topic you have seen least.
5. **No repeats** until every post has been shown once.
6. **Why.** For every post, we can show the reasons. Nothing is secret.
7. **Mirror.** The topic with the most points is what the feed thinks you care about.

**Rewrite Challenge:** the feed starts out stuck on Appearance. In 12 actions, make the next 10 posts have 20% or less Appearance, and at least 3 topics with 15% or more.

**Remember:** a feed is a mirror of what we stop on. We can choose what we stop on.
